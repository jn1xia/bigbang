    Fan-made and unofficial; not affiliated with YG Entertainment, PK Entertainment or Jakarta International Stadium. Categories, prices and where each category sits follow the official seat map (prices exclude 10% tax and the 6% platform fee). Exact block edges, section numbers, row counts and stage sizes are estimates, so use the official map to confirm your seat. The stadium shell uses JIS's published figures: three tiers raked at 24°, 29° and 32°, about 82,000 seats, and a retractable roof.
